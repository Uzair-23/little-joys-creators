  • Attention vs Sales Split (Hindi Language): Ads in Hindi generate a substantial positive lift in Hook Rate (+0.1049, 95% CI [0.0182, 0.1320], adj p = 0.0400).   • Child in Opening Frame (+0.0809 Hook Lift): Placing a child in the first 3 seconds drives a robust, reproducible +0.0809 lift in Hook Rate (95% CI [0.0484, 0.1138], adj p = 0.0016).

6. What This Data Cannot Tell You: Critical Limitations

  • No Rate Cards or Commercial Cost Data: The dataset contains zero pricing information. We cannot claim cost-efficiency or customer acquisition cost (CAC) superiority.
  • Inferred Audience Demographics for 17 Creators: Audience export data exists for only 3 of 20 creators (CR-14, CR-15, CR-20). All demographic claims for the other 17 accounts are strictly inferred.
  • Observational Associations, Not Causes: All ad findings demonstrate statistical association within past campaigns, not guaranteed causal lift.
  • AI-Generated Label Error Rates: Human audit revealed measurable error rates in automated tags (16.7% on hook types, 8.3% on ad format and child presence).
  • Organic vs Sponsored Delivery Discount: Creator sponsored posts average lower than organic median plays. Sponsored performance will lag viral organic benchmarks.

7. Before You Sign Anyone: Actionable Marketing Checklist

  1. Request Creator-Side Demographics: Obtain native platform analytics screenshots verifying women and India geographic audience proportions.
  2. Obtain Commercial Rate Cards: Negotiate 12-month paid digital advertising amplification rights across Meta and Google ad libraries.
  3. Mandatory Legal & Regulatory Review: Have compliance counsel clear all script formulations against ASCI Food & Beverage Code and FSSAI (2018) regulations.
  4. Strict Exclusivity Lockout: Enforce 90-day non-compete clauses prohibiting endorsements of competing kids' nutrition brands (e.g., Gritzo).
  5. Colloquial Native-Speaker Run-Through: Conduct rapid verbal run-throughs of Hindi/Hinglish scripts to guarantee authentic, natural cadence.
