## 7. Do's and Don'ts (Creator Authenticity Guidelines)

### Do (What Feels Native):
A sponsored post must feel like a clinical consultation or a myth-busting segment. It should feature the creator in his professional attire, using text overlays to highlight key medical takeaways or product benefits.

### Don't (What Would Feel Foreign):
High-energy, influencer-style transitions, overly casual slang, or a lack of medical context would feel out of place. A script that reads like a generic advertisement rather than a doctor's recommendation will break trust.
