## 7. Do's and Don'ts (Creator Authenticity Guidelines)

### Do (What Feels Native):
A video filmed in a casual home setting using natural light, featuring the creator and her daughter in a candid or humorous interaction. The script should feel like a spontaneous observation of family life rather than a rehearsed advertisement.

### Don't (What Would Feel Foreign):
High-production studio lighting, overly formal or scripted language, and a lack of the creator's daughter in the frame. Any content that feels like a traditional television commercial will break the trust established by her candid, home-video aesthetic.
